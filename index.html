Help & Guide

What this does

Paste AI output on the left. The right pane shows a cleaned, human-typed style version. Cleanup happens live as you type or toggle filters. Your choices are saved in your browser.

Processing order

  1. Always-on cleanup: trim trailing spaces, convert smart quotes.
  2. Filters (checkboxes): bold/italic/code/links, blank lines, etc.
  3. Custom replacements: your own “find → replace” rules (order preserved).

Note: rule order matters in the custom table—entries run top to bottom.

Filters

  • TB – remove blank lines at the very start and end
  • CB – collapse 2+ blank lines to one
  • B – strip **bold** / __bold__ (strict Markdown)
  • I – strip *italic* / _italic_ (strict Markdown)
  • C – strip `inline code`
  • L₁ – convert [text](url) → text
  • L₂ – convert [text](url) → url

Display settings

Cautions

  • Over-filtering: Please double-check the output when you paste complex strings or code snippets.
  • Backup: Export the mapping JSON and keep it for later use. Feel free to download a useful example here.
